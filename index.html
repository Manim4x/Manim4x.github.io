Our Story

Heritage Craftsmanship
Meets Modern Precision

Sathiyam Constructions was founded in 2001 with one unwavering commitment — to build structures that honour tradition while meeting the demands of the modern world. Every project we undertake carries the weight of our legacy and the pride of our craft.

With deep expertise in Eco-Friendly Houses, Residential Buildings, Hotels, Sacred Temples, and Farmhouses — our master craftsmen carry forward generations of Southern India's finest architectural traditions, blending timeless techniques with modern precision.

What We Build

Our Specialisations

01
Eco-Friendly Houses
Eco-friendly houses are homes designed and built using sustainable materials and energy-efficient methods that minimize harm to the environment, reducing carbon footprint and conserving natural resources like water and energy.
02
Hotels
Boutique hotels and resorts that marry heritage aesthetics with modern hospitality — open courtyards, sloped tiled roofs, and raw stone facades.
03
Temples
Sacred structures built with devotion — following traditional Thachu Shastra principles, granite carvings, and time-honoured temple architecture.
04
Farm House
A farmhouse is a residential building located on agricultural or rural land, originally built to house farmers and their families while providing easy access to their fields and livestock.
